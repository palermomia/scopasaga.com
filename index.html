How it plays

If you know Scopa, you already know how. If you don't, the first levels teach you in a minute.

Capture by adding up

Play a card to take table cards that add up to its value. A 7 takes a 3 and a 4, or another 7.

Sweep for a Scopa

Clear the whole table in one move for a Scopa and a big bonus. Grab the Settebello, the 7 of coins, when you can.

Hundreds of levels

Card piles, lemons, chained cards and the wild Jolly keep every level fresh, with new twists in every region.

Weekly leagues

Climb from Terracotta to the Settebello league. The top ten move up every week.

Helpers from Nonna

Reshuffle your hand, sweep away a card, or ask Nonna for the best move when you're stuck.

Beautiful old-world cards

A deck drawn in the style of 19th-century Italian printed cards, on an aged-paper finish.

A journey up the boot

Start at the Quattro Canti in Palermo and travel north, one town at a time.
