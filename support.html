How to play

How do captures work?

Tap a card in your hand. It takes table cards that add up to its value: a 7 can take a 3 and a 4. If a card on the table has the same value as yours, you must take that one. If several captures are possible, the cards you can take light up; tap the ones you want.

What is a Scopa?

Clearing every card from the table in one move. It's worth a big bonus, and some levels ask you to make one or more.

What do the card values mean?

Number cards are worth their number. The Fante (page) is 8, the Cavallo (knight) is 9 and the Re (king) is 10. The Settebello is the 7 of coins.

How do I get more stars?

Finish the goals with cards to spare. Every card left in your hand and deck adds bonus points at the end.

Progress and leagues

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device and on our servers. Deleting the app may remove progress stored on the device.

How do leagues work?

Each week you're placed in a group of up to 50 players. Winning levels earns league points. When the week ends (Monday, 00:00 UTC), the top 10 move up a league and the bottom 10 move down.

Ads

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Scopa Saga free. Most ads are optional: you choose to watch a video for extra cards or a free helper.

A video didn't give me my reward.

Rewards are given when a video plays to the end. If that happened and you didn't get it, email us with the time and what you were trying to get.

Your data

How do I delete my data?

In the game, open Settings → Delete my data. This permanently removes your account and progress. You can also email us. See our Privacy Policy.
